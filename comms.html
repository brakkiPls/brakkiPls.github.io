How to Comission!


Hooray! You decided that this is cool enough and you would like an art? Here is how it goes:

1. Contact me!
Pick any of the listed Contact Information and send me a message there! We will talk about your Idea and what you want, and if i am willing to draw it, i will ask you for an E-Mail, to send an invoice to!
Check out my TOS and my Queue before reaching out, please!

2. Start!
Once you paid the invoice, you will be put on my Queue, where you can see the progress in text form i will have made on your art!

3. Updates!
Over time, i will send you progress updates! You will recieve an update once i drew out the rough idea and once i finished the flat colours. After each of these steps, you are encouraged to give me feedback on what you think of the art. After both these iterations are approved by you, i will finish the art.

4. Completion!
I will leave myself a total of up to 1 month to finish an art, since i work pretty slowly! Feel free to poke me at any time to remind me to work on it tho! I am really bad about remembering!
Once the art is done, you will recieve the full resolution image and a low resolution image to share!

5. After!
You're encouraged to post the art and tag me, if you're happy with it! It really helps me as an artist to get more eyes on my work! I may post it as well, if i am particularly proud of my work!
